Welcome to Longwood

Reputedly New Zealand’s largest private home, Longwood welcomes you in the finest English country-house tradition — grand rooms, open fires and gracious hospitality, wrapped in gardens a century in the making.

The House

One of the country’s grandest homes

Longwood, named after Napoleon’s house of exile on St Helena, was taken up as a sheep station in the 1850's by Henry Bunny who built a small house on the property and established the gardens. In 1906 it burned down and was replaced with a brick house designed by John Swan. This house was subsequently remodelled by William Gray Young in 1921. The style is English-asymmetrical in the manner of Lutyens, with a gracious neo-Georgian interior of grand staircases, ornate ceilings, Adam fireplaces and fine wood panelling.

Four large ensuite bedrooms wait upstairs with canopied beds, antique furnishings and open fires. Four more bedrooms are available for larger groups or small conferences. Savour dinner in the glorious dining room, relax in the library, or view the stars from the balcony or loggia.

The Grounds

A swimming pool, tennis court and the estate’s historic stables sit among grounds and outbuildings that carry Heritage New Zealand’s highest status.

The Region

The heart of the South Wairarapa

Longwood lies a little over an hour from Wellington and minutes from Martinborough and Greytown. Martinborough is 15 minutes, Greytown is 8 minutes. The South Wairarapa is a region renowned for world-class Pinot Noir, boutique wineries, gourmet food, excellent shopping and a vibrant arts scene. Beyond the vines are wild coastlines, the Putangirua Pinnacles and the Cape Palliser seal colony.
