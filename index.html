Welcome to Longwood

Reputedly New Zealand’s largest private home, Longwood welcomes you in the finest English country-house tradition — grand rooms, open fires and gracious hospitality, wrapped in gardens a century in the making.

The House

One of the country’s grandest homes

Built in 1906 by architect William Gray Young and named after Napoleon’s house of exile on St Helena, Longwood is English-asymmetrical in the manner of Lutyens, with a gracious neo-Georgian interior of grand staircases, ornate ceilings and fine wood panelling.

Four large ensuite bedrooms wait upstairs with canopied beds, antique furnishings and open fires. By evening, savour dinner in the glorious dining room, the library, or the loggia.

The Grounds

Vast lawns, venerable trees, quiet corners

Wander park-like grounds shaded by venerable English trees, retire to the Sunken Garden with a book, or take to the lawn for tennis, croquet and boules. A swimming pool, tennis court and the estate’s historic stables sit among grounds and outbuildings that carry Heritage New Zealand’s highest status.

Meetings & Retreats

A considered setting for small conferences

Trade the boardroom for a heritage country house an hour from Wellington. Longwood lends itself to board meetings, strategy days and creative retreats — the panelled rooms make for focused sessions, while the gardens, loggia and open fires draw people together between them.

Take the estate exclusively for the day or stay the night: with on-site accommodation, catering by arrangement and Martinborough’s wineries at the gate, a small conference here feels a world away from the office.

The Region

The heart of the South Wairarapa

Longwood lies a little over an hour from Wellington and minutes from Martinborough — a region renowned for world-class Pinot Noir, boutique wineries, gourmet food and a vibrant arts scene. Beyond the vines are wild coastlines, the Putangirua Pinnacles and the Cape Palliser seal colony.
